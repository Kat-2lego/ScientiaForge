Behind the Intelligence

Decoding the Job Market

We are a team of engineers, ex-recruiters, and data scientists on a mission to democratize elite career coaching.

The Gap We Saw.
The Bridge We Built.

Founded in Johannesburg, SkillForge AI was born out of a deep frustration with the modern hiring process. We noticed a disturbing trend: highly skilled candidates were being rejected not because they lacked ability, but because they didn't know how to speak the language of algorithmic ATS filters and corporate recruiters.

Traditional career coaching is expensive, and generic internet advice is outdated. We realized that by leveraging advanced Large Language Models, we could provide personalized, data-driven career guidance to anyone, anywhere, at a fraction of the cost.
