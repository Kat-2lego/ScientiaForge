SKILLFORGE AI 
1. Project Overview (What is SkillForge AI?) 
SkillForge AI is a smart career guidance platform that helps people make better career 
decisions, improve job applications, and prepare for interviews using AI-powered tools. 
It is designed to support users from: 
• Choosing a career 
• Building strong CVs 
• Practicing interviews 
• Identifying missing skills 
• Preparing for better job opportunities 

Simple idea of the app: 
It is a career coach in your pocket. 

2. Why This Project Matters (Problem We Are Solving) 
In South Africa, many young people struggle to find jobs even after studying. 
The main problems are: 
• Many students are unsure about career choices 
• CVs are often poorly written or not ATS-friendly 
• Job interviews are intimidating and unfamiliar 
• People do not know what skills employers want 
• Career guidance is limited or not personalised 

The result: 
Qualified people remain unemployed or underprepared for work. 
SkillForge AI solution: 
We bring all career tools into one simple platform that guides users step-by-step.

    4. Key Insights (Research Findings) 
        From market research and general career trends: 
        • Most graduates are not fully job-ready 
        • Employers care about: 
        • Communication skills 
        • Practical skills 
        • Good CV presentation 
        • Career guidance is often: 
        • Not personalised 
        • Not easily accessible 
        • Students use multiple apps for different needs: 
        • One for CVs 
        • One for jobs 
        • One for learning 

Main gap: There is no single platform that combines all career preparation tools in one place. 

    5. What the App Does (Core Features) 

        SkillForge AI is built around 4 main tools: 

        1. Career Discovery Tool 
            • Suggests possible careers based on interests and subjects 
            • Helps users understand “what can I become?” 
        2. CV Improvement Tool 
            • Reviews CVs 
            • Gives a simple score 
            • Suggests improvements (layout, wording, keywords) 
        3. Interview Practice Tool 
            • Simulates real interview questions 
            • Gives feedback on: 
            • Confidence 
            • Clarity 
            • Structure of answers 
        4. Skill Gap Tool 
            • Compares current skills with job requirements 
            • Shows what skills are missing 
            • Suggests what to learn next
