1Important notice about CVs and personal details

Please do not include personal details that are not needed for CV analysis. This means your ID or passport number, full home street address, date of birth, photograph, marital status, race, religion, health or disability information, criminal record, bank or card details, passwords, private contact details of referees, or any information about other people.

The reason is that our AI Features work by sending the text of your CV and other Content to third-party AI Providers, which process it on their own systems, possibly outside South Africa. Those providers have their own terms. Depending on the provider and service tier, they may keep inputs for a period and may use them to improve or train their models. Once your Content has been sent, we cannot recall it or control what the provider does with it. See section 6 and clause 8 of our Terms of Service.

The safest CV to upload contains only your name (or initials), a professional email address, your city and province, your education, your skills and your work experience.

3Personal information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: name and surname, email address, password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), age bracket, gender, and your account role.
  • Profile and career information: questionnaire answers, school subjects and marks you choose to enter, grade or education level, courses and careers of interest, preferred provinces, skills, goals and saved career paths.
  • CV content: CV files you upload, text extracted from them, and the scores, skill lists, gaps and suggestions generated from them.
  • Practice sessions: your answers in the Interview Simulator and Articulation Coach, speech you record and its transcript, and the feedback and scores generated.
  • Applications: job titles, employers, deadlines, status, notes, job descriptions you paste and the match results.
  • Study activity: papers you open, active-recall answers and results, and progress records.
  • Payments: the plan or feature you bought, amount, status, date, reference number and type of payment method. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages you send to support and your preferences.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: the features you use, when you use them, how long you spend on each (for example in the Interview Simulator), actions taken and quotas used.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, operating system, language, timestamps, and log and error data.
  • Cookies and browser storage: see section 13.

3.3 Information from others

We may receive payment confirmations from our payment provider and, for Minors, confirmation of consent from a parent or guardian.

3.4 Special personal information

POPIA gives extra protection to special personal information, such as race or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, health, sex life, trade-union membership, political persuasion, criminal behaviour and biometric information. We do not ask for it and do not need it. We do not use your voice to identify you. If you include special personal information in a CV or other Content despite our warning, section 6 and clause 8.4 of our Terms of Service apply, and we will use it only to provide the feature you asked for.

4Why we use your information

We use personal information only for specific, lawful purposes. For each, the lawful ground under POPIA is shown in brackets.

  • To provide the Platform and its features (performance of our agreement with you; your consent).
  • To create and secure your account, including sign-in and verification (performance of our agreement; legitimate interest).
  • To process payments and keep financial records, and to prevent fraud (performance of our agreement; legal obligation; legitimate interest).
  • To run the AI Features: analysing CVs, generating interview and speaking feedback, matching you to careers and jobs (performance of our agreement; your consent).
  • To send service messages, such as security alerts, receipts, deadline and follow-up reminders, and notifications in the Platform (performance of our agreement; legitimate interest).
  • To keep the Platform safe and fair, for example to enforce usage limits and detect misuse (legitimate interest).
  • To understand and improve the Platform, including studying which features are used and for how long, and producing aggregated statistics (such as age-bracket and gender breakdowns on our internal dashboards) that do not identify you (legitimate interest).
  • To give support and respond to requests and complaints (legitimate interest; performance of our agreement).
  • To comply with law, respond to lawful requests and protect our legal rights (legal obligation; legitimate interest).
  • To send you marketing, but only if you have opted in (your consent).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertising on the Platform. We do not use your Content to profile you for advertising.

Where we rely on your consent (for example to process your CV with AI Providers, or to send marketing), you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us or using your account settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, but we may then be unable to provide the related feature.

6AI processing and automated analysis

6.1What is sent. When you use an AI Feature, we send the Content needed for that feature to an AI Provider. This can include the text of your CV, your interview and speaking answers or transcripts, questionnaire answers, and job descriptions. We aim to send only what each feature needs.

6.2AI Providers. We currently use providers such as Google (Gemini) and Groq, and may add or change providers. They process your Content for us under their own terms and our arrangements with them. Depending on their terms and the plan we use, they may retain inputs for a time (for example for security and abuse monitoring) and may use inputs to improve their services and models. We do not control this.

6.3Our own use. As at the date of this Policy, we do not use your Content to train or build our own AI models. If that changes, we will tell you in advance and ask for your consent where the law requires.

6.4No solely automated decisions with legal effect. CV scores, match percentages, career suggestions and similar results are information to help you. We do not use them to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant consequences. You may ask us to explain a result or for a person to review a concern (section 12).

6.5Limits of AI. Results can be wrong or biased. Please treat them as guidance and verify important information independently.

6.6What you can do. Remove unnecessary personal details before uploading, avoid saying personal details during voice practice, delete CVs you no longer want held in your account, and contact us with any concern.

By using the AI Features you acknowledge that your Content will be processed outside South Africa.

10How we protect information

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access and unlawful processing, including:

  • encryption of data in transit (HTTPS) and encrypted storage by our cloud providers;
  • hashed passwords and secure authentication;
  • access controls, including row-level security and role-based administrator access;
  • keeping secret keys and credentials on the server rather than in your browser;
  • monitoring and logging, limiting staff access, and reviewing our providers.

No system is completely secure. If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA requires. You can help by using a strong, unique password, not sharing your login, and signing out on shared devices.
