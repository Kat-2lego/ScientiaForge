Behind the Intelligence

Decoding the Job Market

We are a team of South African IT students building an AI-powered career coach to help job seekers present their skills with confidence.

The Gap We Saw.
The Bridge We Built.

ScientiaForge AI started in Johannesburg as a student project. We noticed that many capable graduates and job seekers struggle to get past CV filters and interviews, not because they lack ability, but because nobody taught them how the process works.

Professional career coaching is expensive and often out of reach. We are building tools that use AI to give personalised CV feedback, interview practice and skill-gap analysis to people who can't afford a coach.

Our Mission

To help job seekers, especially first-time applicants and graduates, understand what employers look for and present their skills with confidence, using accessible AI-powered tools.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted career companion for South Africans, where good opportunities go to people based on their skills and potential.

Our DNA

The Principles We Code By

Algorithmic Fairness

We aim to give fair, unbiased advice to every candidate, regardless of background.

Radical Transparency

No black-box coaching. We explain exactly why our AI suggests a specific keyword or interview tactic.

Human-Centric

AI is the tool; you are the talent. We build technology to amplify your unique voice, not to replace it with a robot's.

Relentless Innovation

The job market keeps changing, so we keep learning and improving the platform.
